The two problems

1. The date parser reads month-first (379 reports). The shared parser reads every date month first. All eleven tenants run day-first (European) locales, so a date like 7/4 (7 April) comes back as 4 July. This is the real #4471. It covers every channel (app 204, email 150, form 25) and every tenant, seen 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-04. Filed as parser · 379 reports.

2. The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY (33 reports). Three tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) run the booking-v3 form, whose date field prints a MM/DD/YYYY hint to day-first users. They type month-first and the form stores it that way: a different bug, in the form, not the parser. Form channel only, seen 2026-04-17 through 2026-09-22. Filed as hint · 33 reports.

Intake's intakeTag guessed wrong both ways: it tagged 23 parser reports as hint and 15 hint reports as parser. The cause is the tenant's locale and form, not intake's guess at the browser.

One-day move (a footnote, not a third ticket)

desk-notes.json flags five reports that read as a one-day shift rather than a month flip (for example "client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday, same meeting").
